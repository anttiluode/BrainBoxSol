What this establishes

The writer has no labels, reward, teacher or adjoint. The calibrated readout is supervised. Each primary arm stores 25 material scalars; global resource normalization and read-phase freezing are explicit controls. Persistent material survives a fast-state reset. This does not establish physical mass, time dilation, noise necessity, autonomous learning, greater capacity or digital efficiency.

The uniform arm also erases the spatial trace. Shuffling preserves its values while breaking their locations. Noise-off changes input power, so its comparison is diagnostic only. The fixed conductance operator is unchanged and its DC solution cannot be repaired by mobility alone.
